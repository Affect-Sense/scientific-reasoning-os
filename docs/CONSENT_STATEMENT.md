# Beta Tester Consent Statement

SROS was tested by four external researchers between 7 and 11 August 2026.
Three provided substantive feedback; the fourth engaged with the system but
submitted no feedback and is not referenced individually in any material.

Ahead of this submission, each feedback-providing tester was contacted
individually and asked, in writing, for permission to use their name, their
role, and a quotation from their feedback in a public competition video.
Two granted explicit written consent: **Dr Gustavo Padrón Rivera
(Universidad de Hidalgo)**, who consented to the use of his name, role,
quotation and original audio recording; and **Dr Guillermo Calderón**, who
consented to the use of the written feedback he had submitted. The third
tester did not give a clear affirmative response, instead returning the
decision to us. We treated that as a declination, confirmed to her in
writing that she would remain unnamed, and did not ask again. She appears in
the submission only as "an established environmental researcher," and no
quotation of hers is attributed.

Consent was sought before any material was used, was not re-solicited after
an ambiguous reply, and no consent was inferred from silence.

Behavioural data — question counts, revision counts, session timings and
agent costs — is drawn from our own system logs, is reported in aggregate,
and contains no personally identifying information.
